# Compilations pourbase cannot include

These compilations hold thermodynamic data that pourbase does not distribute, because their licence
forbids redistribution, they are commercial, or no redistribution grant could be identified. They are
listed so that users interested in a particular element can consult them directly. Values found there
can be used locally through own species (`pourbase.with_user_species`, or File → Load own species in the
desktop application) without changing the distributed database.

pourbase holds no values copied from these compilations. Where pourbase uses a value that a compilation
also lists, it comes from an independent open route, such as the original publication or an openly
licensed evaluation, and that route is the cited source.

| Compilation | Scope | Where to find it | Why it is not included |
|---|---|---|---|
| CODATA Key Values for Thermodynamics (Cox, Wagman and Medvedev, 1989) | Internationally agreed key values for elements, ions and simple compounds | [codata.info](https://codata.info/resources/databases/key1.html); book, Hemisphere (1989) | Commercially published compilation. Key values reproduced in other compilations, such as the bold CODATA entries of NEA auxiliary tables, are excluded as well; the NEA and NBS evaluations used here are consistent with them as auxiliary data |
| Thermal Constants of Substances, TKV (Glushko et al.) | About 27,000 substances, including aqueous ions; 1 atm standard state | [Online edition, Moscow State University](https://www.chem.msu.ru/cgi-bin/tkv.pl?show=welcome.html); English edition, Begell House (1999) | The online edition prohibits copying and reposting its materials |
| IVTANTHERMO | Evaluated database of gases and condensed substances, mainly at high temperature | [Description (PDF)](https://en.iric.imet-db.ru/PDF/11.pdf)¹ | Distribution terms not confirmed |
| Gurvich et al., Thermodynamic Properties of Individual Substances, 4th ed. | Gases and condensed phases of the elements and their compounds | Book, Hemisphere (1989–1994) | Copyrighted compilation; no redistribution grant |
| Barin, Thermochemical Data of Pure Substances, 3rd ed. (1995) | Pure solids, liquids and gases | Book, VCH (1995), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/9783527619825) | Copyrighted compilation; no redistribution grant |
| Naumov, Ryzhenko and Khodakovsky, Handbook of Thermodynamic Data (English translation, 1974) | Inorganic solids, liquids, gases and aqueous species | [EPA HERO record](https://hero.epa.gov/reference/8215113/); library or NTIS | No open full text or reuse terms found |
| CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics | Thermochemical tables for elements and compounds | [Online edition](https://hbcp.chemnetbase.com/) | Commercial, copyrighted handbook |
| Restricted NIST Standard Reference Databases, such as SRD 46 (Critically Selected Stability Constants of Metal Complexes) | Equilibrium constants of metal complexes | [nist.gov/srd/nist46](https://www.nist.gov/srd/nist46) | Licence does not allow redistribution. (Openly released NIST data, such as the digitized NBS Tables, are used.) |
| JAEA-TDB | Aqueous reactions, solids and auxiliary data for radionuclides and geochemistry | [migrationdb.jaea.go.jp](https://migrationdb.jaea.go.jp/tdb_e2/tdb_pre_e.html) (registration) | Non-profit use with attribution; redistribution and modification of the database files are forbidden |
| Thermoddem (BRGM) | Minerals, aqueous species and gases for geochemical modelling | [thermoddem.brgm.fr](https://thermoddem.brgm.fr/)¹ | No redistribution grant identified |
| ThermoChimie | Radionuclides and cement and clay systems | [thermochimie-tdb.com](https://www.thermochimie-tdb.com/) | Data terms and exact version unresolved |
| MALT (Kagaku Gijutsu-Sha) | About 7,900 species including aqueous species, with Pourbaix calculations | [kagaku.com/malt](https://www.kagaku.com/malt/index_en.html)¹ | Commercial licensed software |
| Ye Dalun, Practical Handbook of Inorganic Thermodynamic Data | Mainly pure inorganic substances | Book, Metallurgical Industry Press; libraries | Reuse terms and editions not confirmed |
| Liang Yingjiao, Handbook of Inorganic Thermodynamic Data | Inorganic substances | Book, Northeastern University Press; libraries | Reuse terms and editions not confirmed |
| Chinese Academy of Sciences chemical databases | Chemical and thermochemical data | [organchem.csdb.cn](https://organchem.csdb.cn/scdb/main/find_db2.htm)¹ | Registration, provenance and reuse terms unclear |
| Databases of commercial software: HSC Chemistry, FactSage, Thermo-Calc, OLI | Thermochemistry for process and materials modelling | [HSC](https://www.mogroup.com/portfolio/hsc-chemistry/), [FactSage](https://www.factsage.com/), [Thermo-Calc](https://thermocalc.com/), [OLI](https://www.olisystems.com/) | Proprietary |

¹ Could not be reached at the time of writing; check the link in a browser.
