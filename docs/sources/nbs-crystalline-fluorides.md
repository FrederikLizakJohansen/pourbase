# Original open NBS crystalline fluorides

R16d3a retains 80 original G/H/S observations on 29 Gibbs-bearing source rows:
25 new crystalline records, nine alternatives on existing NEA fluorides and
71 newly available properties. Three original NaF cells already retained are
linked without duplication. Four source blanks remain explicit. All earlier
preferred values and 31 fixed references remain.

The source is Wagman et al. (1982), *The NBS Tables of Chemical Thermodynamic
Properties*, digitized with errata in explicitly open non-SRD
[NIST M32124](https://doi.org/10.18434/M32124). Exact logical CSV records,
columns, raw values, source phase labels, units, conditions and reuse evidence
remain in each observation. NIST is acknowledged and data are distributed
without warranty under its recorded open terms. Completed source/rights
reviews carry forward; these additions use original NBS cells.

G/H are stored in J/mol, S in J/(mol K), at 298.15 K and 1 bar per complete
formula unit. Missing uncertainty remains NULL. The critical table's underlying
measurement versus estimate classification is unspecified for these cells.
Class B describes the critically evaluated compilation. Structured evaluation
and derivation identities identify each original table row and SI conversion.

Hydrates retain all water once, including CuF2·2H2O, AgF·2H2O, AgF·4H2O,
NiF2·4H2O and KF·2H2O. The source supplies G alone for the copper dihydrate
and nickel tetrahydrate. No H/S is inferred. Fluoride is externally buffered
using the unchanged free reference. These records describe the primary element
inventory; closed fluoride, concentrated brine, water activity and new thermal
component models remain separate.

The source beta, monoclinic ZrF4 maps to the existing source-resolved beta
phase. Original monoclinic HfF4 and alpha-quartz BeF2 labels remain visible;
new exact structure assignments remain unresolved. The distinct source
beta-cristobalite BeF2 enthalpy is queued with the remaining 35 rows.

CrF3 has a source-cycle residual of −1217.9925 J/mol against the original NBS
elemental references. The printed original Table 51:Cr, p.2-198 (PDF p.206),
confirms G −1088 kJ/mol, H298 −1159 kJ/mol and S 93.89 J/(mol K).
All three remain, with an explicit consistency warning. The separate 0 K
enthalpy is outside this import. No numerical correction or uncertainty is
inferred from the discrepancy.
