# Six crystalline formation-energy gaps

The 25 October 2026 screen covers all **44 tracked datasets**: nine legacy
ThermoHub exports, eight SUPCRT variants, legacy and current NASA datasets,
14 PHREEQC files, original MINES23.1, the open non-SRD NBS release and nine
ThermoHub v1.2.0 graph families. All 23 graph collections retain their verified
R14 hashes. The 27 composition-matching property rows provide **no verified
neutral crystalline target**. All six experimental G gaps remain open:
Au₂O₃, In(OH)₃, NiO₂, PdO₂, PtO and PtO₂.

Each gap requires a source-defined crystalline phase, neutral charge and a
formation Gibbs energy applicable at 298.15 K and 1 bar. Existing Materials
Project estimates retain their separate computational-fallback status.
Source formulas, phase labels and original evaluation ancestry remain essential
when considering a replacement.

The retrieved 2019 indium author manuscript carries **CC BY-NC-ND 3.0**.
Its introduction gives an adopted NIST 46.7 log Ksp of −36.9, while its conclusion
prints −39.9. The independent measurements, phase identity and discrepancy
need their own source review. The manuscript licence and its inherited NIST
input supply no open GPL data grant. The 2024 paper's CC BY statement applies
to that paper and its original work.
