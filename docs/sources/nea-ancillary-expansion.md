# Further NEA 2024 ancillary observations

This batch adds **63 observations on 23 Al/Mg/Ca records**, including **4 new
species** and **9 missing properties**: 2 G, 2 S and 5 H. Totals are **1,330
species / 4,153 observations**, with selected G/S/H **1109/1086/906**.
Every earlier observation remains. The unchanged policy selects 54 new preferred
observations in place of earlier candidates; every new observation is class A.

The source is Rand, Palmer, Fuger and Gajda (2024), *Selected Ancillary
Compounds of Interest to Radioactive Waste Management*, Chemical Thermodynamics
15, OECD Nuclear Energy Agency, NEA No. 7626,
[Table III-1, pp. 45–46](https://www.oecd-nea.org/upload/docs/application/pdf/2024-06/cts15-selected_ancillary_compounds-r-final-rev.pdf).
Original PDF pages 87–88 were visually checked, including bold typography,
charge, polymorph labels, values, uncertainty and the G/H/S column order.
Chapter V supplies the critical evaluations and phase definitions.

The supplied NEA clarification and OECD data terms cover these selected NEA
evaluations, with OECD/NEA attribution and the same acknowledgment requirement
passed to sublicensees and further sublicensees. The original table identifies
**bold cells as directly copied CODATA Key Values**. Those cells retain their
separate source hold: this batch excludes Al³⁺/Mg²⁺ H and the Al₂O₃, MgO and
CaO G/H/S rows. The revised, nonbold NEA G/S cells for Al³⁺ and Mg²⁺ retain
their own evaluation provenance. A new NEA calculation's internal auxiliary
references remain documented; no bold auxiliary cell is independently imported.

All observations refer to **298.15 K and 0.1 MPa (1 bar)**, with aqueous
standard properties at infinite dilution. G/H are converted from kJ/mol to
J/mol; S retains J/(mol K). Quoted total uncertainty widths correspond in
principle to approximately **95% confidence**. Their meaning remains explicit;
they are not converted to one-sigma errors or inferred from printed precision.

The magnesium sulfate names follow the NEA discussion, pp. 231–233. Its α
phase has space group Cmcm; β has Pbnm. Some older authors reverse the
nomenclature. The earlier generic MgSO₄ record keeps its identity and values;
its assignment to either named NEA polymorph remains unresolved. NEA increases
the β entropy uncertainty because the original calorimetric sample lacked
structural data. Bayerite and α-MgSO₄ have no selected G/S in this table, so
those properties remain missing. The newly recorded bayerite gap makes the
strict Al completeness check incomplete. Diagrams using available data remain
available; the strict periodic-screen total becomes 55 experimental systems
(58 with the existing computational fallback policy).

Each observation retains a complete source formula unit. Gypsum has two waters
counted once; beta-calcium-phosphate retains three Ca atoms and two phosphate
components before normalization per mole of primary metal. Chloride, sulfate,
carbonate and phosphate use the existing independent free-reference buffers.
Closed balances conserve primary Al, Mg or Ca; secondary activities remain
externally prescribed. Mixing these evaluations with pourbase references does
not reproduce the source's unchanged internally consistent reaction dataset.

Validation covers **123 energies, 19 diagram scenarios and 76 primary-metal
balances**, including ideal/Davies calculations. Across all 75 default systems,
changes occur only in Al, Mg and Ca: 182/91/91 stable-grid points, respectively,
on the 81×91 grid. Uncertainty-mask changes are retained in the validation
record. The original Ni/Sn/Fe choices remain. For Al³⁺, the existing temperature
model now uses the selected NEA S from the same source as G, ahead of H from
a different source. The earlier calorimetric H and derived Al–F observations
remain retained with their original anchors. The independent cycle diagnostic
against older open NBS elemental entropies differs by at most **0.169 kJ/mol**;
ionic checks include the conventional proton entropy reference. This diagnostic
supplies no correction or new uncertainty.

Remaining Si/P/B, mixed-component and carbonate ancillary selections continue
as separate numerical batches. R18 retains Cp, thermal, pressure, activity and
covariance work. All earlier source-specific holds remain. Historical requested
coverage is **116/131**, and the six original crystalline G gaps remain open.
