# Remaining NEA ancillary formation observations

This batch adds **60 observations across 24 source records**, including **12
species** and **26 missing properties**: 12 G, 7 S and 7 H. Totals are **1,342
species / 4,213 observations**, with selected G/S/H **1121/1093/913**.
The unchanged policy selects 33 new preferred observations in place of earlier
candidates. Every earlier alternative remains stored.

The source is Rand, Palmer, Fuger and Gajda (2024), *Selected Ancillary
Compounds of Interest to Radioactive Waste Management*, Chemical Thermodynamics
15, OECD Nuclear Energy Agency, NEA No. 7626,
[Table III-1, pp. 44–47](https://www.oecd-nea.org/upload/docs/application/pdf/2024-06/cts15-selected_ancillary_compounds-r-final-rev.pdf).
The original table pages were visually checked for values, uncertainty,
charge, phase and bold typography. Chapter V supplies the original critical
selections and source identity. The nonbold phosphate row retains its original
own NEA 1992 evaluation, reprinted here; it is new to this database.

Every observation retains **298.15 K and 0.1 MPa (1 bar)**, with aqueous
standard properties at infinite dilution. Source G/H values in kJ/mol are
converted to J/mol; S retains J/(mol K). Quoted total uncertainty widths
correspond in principle to approximately 95% confidence. Their meaning remains
explicit, with no conversion to one sigma or inferred missing uncertainty.

Full formula units are essential. A cyclic H₃Si₃O₉³⁻ record retains three Si
atoms and charge −3; reducing the formula would change the species and its
concentration law. The source colemanite formula CaB₃O₄(OH)₃·H₂O and inyoite
formula CaB₃O₃(OH)₅·4H₂O are half their conventional Ca₂B₆ units. The stored
numbers follow those original CaB₃ units. Structural OH and hydrate waters
are counted once. Secondary silica, calcium, carbonate, phosphate and fluoride
references remain externally prescribed; closed balances conserve primary Si,
P, B, Mg or Ca.

The existing override keeps **H₃SiO₄⁻ G = −1252304 J/mol** from Miron.
The independent NEA **−1253236 ± 1034 J/mol** is retained as class A and remains
available for selection. The override's original reason concerns variants of
one evaluation; its continued application to an independent NEA source is an
explicit policy-review follow-up. No preference override was changed.

All six G-only oligomer rows retain missing H/S and are excluded from thermal
calculations. Existing supported nonligand thermal calculations use compatible
source G/H according to the established model. This batch introduces no new
Cp, HKF, pressure, activity, solution or coupled secondary-inventory model.

Validation covers **126 energies, 17 diagrams, 68 primary-metal balances and
16 source thermal-derivative checks**. The earlier free H₂SiO₄²⁻
and orthosilicic-acid component references retain their original fixed values;
new preferred species evaluations do not silently replace those model anchors.
Earlier local derivations remain checked against their original inputs. All 75 default systems are compared;
changes are confined to Si, P and B. Source-cycle diagnostics include the
conventional ionic proton reference and differ by at most **0.281 kJ/mol**
against older open NBS elemental entropies. Reported source numbers remain
unchanged; that diagnostic supplies no new uncertainty or correction.

The supplied NEA clarification and OECD terms cover these own selected
evaluations. OECD and NEA are acknowledged. Distributions pass the same OECD
acknowledgment requirement to sublicensees and require further sublicensees
to do the same. Other sources' permission holds remain separate.
Historical requested coverage remains **116/131**; the six original crystalline
G gaps remain open.
